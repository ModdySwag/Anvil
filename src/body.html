Levels

Both knobs ride the master tap: Instrument sets the keys bus (what the instrument plays), Master is the final out. Drag or twist the rim · wheel steps · arrow keys when focused · double-click resets.

Synth

Play it from the piano dock below or your MIDI keys.

FX Rack

The rack lands here — five modules inside the mixer worklet: drive → mod → delay → verb → dyn. All off = bit-identical bypass.

Mod Matrix

The matrix lands here — 8 macro knobs and up to 32 routing slots (env / LFO / velocity / key / rand → cutoff, reso, pitch, osc levels, pan).

MIDI

The hardware deck lands here — enable Web MIDI and your keys play Anvil through the same note path as the piano dock.

Drums

Kit pads land here — set the ENGINE select to DRUM and play the pads, the dock, or MIDI (notes below the split go to the kit).

Samples

The sample core lands here — import WAVs, see the waveform, preview through the master tap.

Recorder

Record the master output and grab a WAV.

Sequencer

The step sequencer lands here — 4 tracks, swing, probability, p-locks, chains and scenes, driving the synth through the one note bus.

Store

The pack store lands here — factory packs download once, verify sha-256 before they are stored, and stay available offline.

Deliver

The Deliver card lands here — one press renders the pattern offline (stems · master · MIDI · README) into a DAW-ready zip. Nothing plays live, nothing is uploaded.
